# BingX: single exchange-side verification, not continuous live trading

For the separate VST autonomous-uncertainty implementation, see
[VST_UNCERTAINTY_RUNTIME.md](VST_UNCERTAINTY_RUNTIME.md). Its persisted BTW
runtime/native-proof tests use an offline read-only fixture; they are not a new
exchange-side test, production recovery claim or REAL write authorization.

The tests for the live executor use simulated responses. The BingX test-order endpoint does not fill an order and cannot prove that a position, a protective stop, or an exit will behave as expected. On 2026-09-26 the owner authorized **one** supervised real test trade, using only isolated margin and at most 15 USDT total test margin including any top-ups. The owner later allowed selecting a previously received `liquidation_cascade` signal, provided any actual entry uses fresh pricing and a new risk assessment; the historical price and timestamp are not current trade authorization. The owner requires every function, including partial takes, breakeven, trailing, margin, cancellation, and emergency exit, to be verified. This is not permission for recurring live trades or for an unmonitored order while the one-shot controller, emergency procedure, and exact-symbol checks are missing. Do not touch existing positions or change account mode. A green test suite does not release the kill switch.

## Before requesting authorization

1. Resolve the entry blocker: determine from the current official API and read-only responses how a newly attached stop obtains a durable *child order ID*. Prove that an active pending stop can be matched to the intended symbol, position side, quantity, trigger price, and specific entry. The parent entry's echoed `stopLoss` is not proof. Use a lossless string `orderID` (or a safe integer); a rounded numeric `orderId` cannot prove identity. Until this works, the real adapter rejects entries before submission.
2. Resolve the stop-move blocker separately. The documented quantity-only `amend` cannot move a trigger; `cancelReplace` can report successful cancellation and failed replacement. Do not run an automated move or a cancel-first experiment with a real position.
3. Select the previously received confirmed `liquidation_cascade` explicitly and preserve its original payload as provenance; do not replay its old price or timestamp. Require the chosen symbol to have no existing exposure or pending orders at entry. Total test margin, including all top-ups, must not exceed 15 USDT; if it requires more or conflicts with existing exposure, stop without automatically choosing another signal. Agree in advance on a fresh price ceiling, stop parameters, slippage risk, who monitors the test, and the manual emergency procedure. Never change account mode or affect unrelated positions.
4. Investigate the documented **Entire Position TP/SL** feature in Hedge Mode as a possible full-position floor: [BingX says it follows reductions and coexists with Partial Position TP/SL](https://bingx.com/en/support/articles/15074354955791). The [swap API](https://github.com/BingX-API/api-ai-skills/blob/main/skills/swap-trade/api-reference.md) documents `STOP_MARKET` with `closePosition=true` but still requires `quantity`; it does **not** establish that this API order has the same lifecycle as the GUI feature, exposes an independently identifiable child after attached entry, or permits a higher trailing stop to coexist with the original floor. Record these questions as explicit risks during the approved supervised test, not as facts proven by the GUI article. A polling trace alone cannot prove coverage during the interval between exchange operations.

## Proposed proof sequence, only after explicit approval

1. Capture read-only baseline: position mode, every position on the chosen symbol, open and conditional orders, available balance, contract precision, and risk limits. Require the chosen symbol to be flat with no pending orders. Do not change account settings.
2. Submit a single uniquely identified entry with a stop, within the approved loss limit. On timeout, **stop immediately**: query by the original client ID and read positions/orders; do not submit another entry.
3. Match the returned order ID, filled status, individual unique fill IDs, BUY side, symbol, timestamps, total executed quantity, live position size and side, liquidation buffer, and the independently identified active stop. If any evidence is missing, stop and use the agreed manual procedure rather than claiming a protected entry.
4. With the position protected and owner approval still valid, first prove that an approved partial exit leaves a verified exchange-side stop for the *actual remaining position*. Then prove a trailing update leaves the prior protective floor active through a failed replacement or timeout; do not simulate this by cancelling the old stop first. Independently match order IDs, SELL fills, filled quantities and the eventual zero remaining position. A partial exit, unrecognized fill, read error, or timeout leaves the intent unknown; never issue a replacement order automatically.
5. Separately test the *read-only reconciliation* of an owner-performed manual close if the owner approves such a scenario. Do not classify an unexplained disappearance as strategy PnL or as a successful exit; inspect liquidation/ADL evidence and reconcile it manually.
6. Keep the kill switch on afterward. Record the evidence and only decide whether to enable any future live path through a separate owner decision.

This document is a plan for one approved, bounded experiment, not an armed order or permission to enable recurring trading. No real order has been submitted under this plan.

## Supervised VST observations (2026-09-27)

The owner separately authorized **virtual VST funds only** and a temporary BTC-USDT leverage change. These observations do not authorize a REAL order. The separate VST host used a VST wallet with a USDT-quoted BTC-USDT contract. LONG and SHORT leverage was temporarily changed from 75x to 10x for the experiments and **restored to 75x on both sides** after BTC-USDT was confirmed flat with no open orders. No REAL account setting or order was changed.

- An initial bounded LIMIT/FOK attempt could not be matched to an exchange order. A later operator-gated reconciliation found the client ID absent from order history, no fills in both VST and USDT queries, no BTC position, and no open orders in two observations after the receive window. Its intent and evidence were archived rather than blindly retrying the same ID.
- A second bounded entry filled 0.0011 BTC with an independently visible STOP_MARKET SELL LONG for the same position ID and full quantity. A verified MARKET emergency exit filled the entire 0.0011 BTC; the stop was cancelled by BingX, leaving no position or open orders.
- A separate bounded entry filled 0.0008 BTC, with one independently visible fixed-quantity STOP_MARKET. A supervised virtual SELL of exactly 0.0002 BTC (25%) filled. Two read-only checks showed the **same stop ID** automatically reduced to the remaining 0.0006 BTC with the same position ID and trigger. This is a VST observation, not a guarantee about mainnet or uninterrupted protection. The stop's `closePosition` was `"false"` even when it tracked the reduction.
- One attempt to add 2 VST of isolated margin was recorded as **unknown**. The subsequently read position margin did not increase as expected. At the time of the attempt, the request used `side=LONG`; the [official endpoint reference](https://github.com/BingX-API/api-ai-skills/blob/main/skills/swap-trade/api-reference.md) specifies `positionSide=LONG`. The adapter was corrected, but the exact cause of that response was not captured and the operation was **not retried**: the API request carries no exchange-side idempotency key. The remaining 0.0006 BTC was closed by a separately verified MARKET emergency exit; the stop was cancelled and the VST symbol was flat with no open orders before leverage restoration.

VST read-only response shapes differed from prior mocks: `allOrders` uses `data.orders`, `allFillOrders` uses `data.fill_orders`; individual fills may have `volume` and an empty `clientOrderID` but a lossless `orderId`. A signed query by `clientOrderId` returned exchange code 109421 even for an order found as FILLED in history. Order history did not echo FOK `timeInForce`. The attached child stop had an empty `mainOrderId` but matched the exact position ID. Reconcile these identities from **multiple** independent reads; do not infer that a parent order's stop-loss echo proves an active stop.

**Still blocked:** No REAL entry, partial-take chain, margin top-up, breakeven move, trailing move, or pre-entry cancellation has been proven end-to-end. In particular, VST auto-resizing a fixed-quantity stop does not establish that cancelling/replacing a trigger never leaves an unprotected interval or that mainnet behaves identically. Keep the REAL kill switch on; do not describe the selected historical `liquidation_cascade` as fresh authorization.

## VST-only execution direction and bounded 1% probe (2026-09-27)

The owner subsequently requested exchange-side execution on **VST virtual funds only**, with **initial isolated margin per trade equal to 1% of the total VST balance**. Necessary margin top-ups may exceed that initial 1%; they must still be calculated and funded before entry, then independently verified after any write. This does **not** authorize a mainnet write. The active signal worker remains PAPER-only while a restart-safe VST strategy executor and safe stop-move procedure are unproven. New VST sizing and exit-policy calculations are preparatory, not an armed trading service; do not route signals to an order-writing worker yet.

- The read-only VST balance was 98,455.6472 VST before a separately guarded BTC-USDT experiment. Its FOK plan used up to 984.556472 VST of **initial** margin; estimated initial margin was approximately 984.41 VST for 0.1155 BTC. This was a **synthetic exchange-mechanics probe**, not a fresh Bullet signal or a complete strategy execution.
- In Hedge Mode, isolated margin was verified. A temporary LONG 10× / SHORT 75× pair was observed. Attempting to change SHORT to 10× was followed by repeated observations of LONG 75× / SHORT 10×; the cause is not established. Both sides were restored to the original 75× / 75× before the separate LONG-only probe. Never assume changing one side leaves the other unchanged: read the entire pair after every setting write.
- The bounded VST FOK entry **filled**. Its returned order ID could not be used as lossless proof, but exact order history, fills, the live position, and an independently visible attached STOP_MARKET were reconciled. The immediate script result was deliberately treated as unknown rather than retrying the entry.
- Because the stop proof remained incomplete, **no second stop or stop move was attempted**. A single explicit emergency SELL filled the entire 0.1155 BTC; the initial stop was canceled with zero fills. The close response was also insufficient for exact ID proof; two delayed independent reads matched its client ID, exact history and fills, a flat BTC-USDT position, and no open BTC-USDT orders. LONG was restored to 75×, leaving both sides at the original 75×. The mainnet account was not changed.
- A second supervised 1% VST FOK intent was stopped by the adapter's final pre-sign check **before POST transport**. Its original generic error did not reveal which check failed; it must not be interpreted as a confirmed order rejection. After a full history window, an exact client-ID query reported not found, history contained no matching order, both VST- and USDT-currency fill reads were empty, and two delayed snapshots found no BTC position or orders. The no-fill intent and leverage-abort evidence were archived separately; LONG was returned to 75× and a later read still showed HEDGE, isolated, both sides 75×, flat BTC and no orders. Subsequent code attaches an allowlisted non-sensitive pre-sign reason to future blocked intents without bypassing any check. The second intent never proceeded to a stop test.

**Still blocked on VST:** The second-stop coexistence test, continuous breakeven/trailing protection, post-fill margin addition, independent core/runner stops, all strategy exits, and automatic processing of fresh signals have not been verified end-to-end. The new 1% sizing policy is **initial margin**, not a guarantee of maximum loss. Do not enable VST automatic entries, and keep the REAL kill switch on.
